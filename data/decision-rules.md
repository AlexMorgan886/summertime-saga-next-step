# Decision Rules

The Next Step Tool uses simple categories to determine which type of resource is most relevant to a player's situation.

## Character Progression

Used when the player is stuck on a specific character or character route.

Category:

`character_progress`

## Story Progression

Used when the player has completed part of the story and is unsure what to do next.

Category:

`story_progress`

## Event or Requirement

Used when a player expects an event to appear but cannot trigger or continue it.

Category:

`event_requirement`

## Location Problem

Used when the player cannot find a location, interaction, or area needed for progression.

Category:

`location_problem`

## General Stuck

Used when the player is unsure what to do next but cannot identify a specific problem.

Category:

`general_stuck`

## Technical Problem

Used when the problem appears to involve installation, saves, updates, compatibility, loading, or another technical issue.

Category:

`technical_problem`

## Important Limitation

These rules recommend the most relevant resource based on the information provided by the player.

They do not determine the player's exact next scene or read their save file.
