# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can the tool tell me the exact next scene?

No. The tool recommends the guide or resource most likely to help based on the information provided. It does not know your exact save state.

## Does the tool read my save file?

No. You do not need to upload or provide a save file.

## Can it determine my exact story progress?

No. It helps narrow down which documentation is most relevant to your situation.

## What if the recommended guide does not solve my problem?

Try the broader walkthrough or another relevant support resource. You can also report an unclear recommendation through the GitHub issue tracker.

## Does the tool modify game files?

No. It only provides a guide recommendation.

## Does it work with mod versions?

The tool's recommendations depend on the information and resources currently included in its data. Version-specific compatibility questions should be checked with the Save Compatibility Checker.
