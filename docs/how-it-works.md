# How the Next Step Tool Works

The SummersagaHub Next Step Tool is designed to help players identify which type of guide is most relevant when they are unsure how to continue.

It is a guide-selection tool, not a save-file analyzer or an exact progression simulator.

## Basic Process

The tool follows a simple process:

```text
Player describes their situation
        ↓
Tool evaluates the supplied information
        ↓
Situation is matched to a category
        ↓
Category is mapped to a relevant resource
        ↓
Player receives a recommended guide
